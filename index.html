THE FIRST YEAR

Small milestones. Big moments.

A simple timeline to help you understand what may happen as baby grows.

0–2 months

Connecting

Faces, sounds, early head lifting and bonding.

2–4 months

Smiling

Social smiles, coos and stronger head control.

4–6 months

Exploring

Reaching, rolling and discovering with hands and mouth.

6–9 months

Moving

Sitting, babbling, recognizing and transferring objects.

9–12 months

Growing

Gestures, pulling to stand and using fingers precisely.

!

Trust your concern. If baby loses skills, struggles to breathe, is very difficult to wake, looks blue/grey, or a young infant has a fever, seek urgent medical care.
