0–12 months

A gentle first-year development timeline

Use milestones as a conversation guide, not a pass/fail test. Children develop at different rates.

0–2 mo

Connecting & settling

Looks at faces, reacts to sounds, moves both arms and legs, briefly lifts head during tummy time.

2–4 mo

Smiles & stronger control

Smiles socially, coos, watches movement, holds head steadier and may bring hands to mouth.

4–6 mo

Reaching & exploring

Laughs, reaches for objects, rolls in one or both directions and explores with hands and mouth.

6–9 mo

Movement & recognition

Responds to name, sits with less support, transfers objects between hands and may begin babbling strings.

9–12 mo

Communication & mobility

Uses gestures, understands familiar words, pulls to stand or cruises, and picks up small items with fingers.

!

Trust your concern. If a baby loses skills they previously had, has trouble breathing, is unusually difficult to wake, looks blue/grey, or a young infant has a fever, seek urgent medical care. For developmental concerns, speak with a qualified child-health professional.
