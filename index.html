baby timeline

First-year milestones made cute and clear.

0–2 mo
🐥

Connecting

Looks at faces, reacts to sound and lifts head briefly.

2–4 mo
😊

Smiling

Smiles socially, coos and holds the head steadier.

4–6 mo
🧸

Exploring

Reaches for objects, rolls and learns through hands and mouth.

6–9 mo
👏

Moving

Sits better, responds to name and transfers objects.

9–12 mo
🚼

Growing

Uses gestures, pulls to stand and picks up tiny objects.

!

Always trust your concern. If a baby looks blue/grey, is hard to wake, has trouble breathing, or a young infant has a fever, seek urgent medical care.
