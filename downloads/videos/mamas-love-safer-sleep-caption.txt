Three basics for safer newborn sleep 🌙

• Place your baby on their back for every sleep, day and night.
• Use a firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet. Keep pillows, loose bedding and toys out of the sleep space.
• Keep your baby’s cot or Moses basket in your room for at least the first 6 months.

Save this reminder and share it with anyone who looks after your baby.

Source: NHS — Reduce the risk of sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS)
