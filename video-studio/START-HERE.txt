MAMA'S LOVE — FREE VIDEO STUDIO

No account, paid API key, or GPU is needed for the free editing workflow.
This makes videos from supplied images. It does not generate new AI photos or realistic moving characters.

MACBOOK SETUP
1. Unzip the complete folder. Keep its files together.
2. Double-click Open Studio.command. If macOS blocks it, right-click the file and choose Open.
3. The editor opens in your browser. Keep the Terminal window open while working.
4. If Python 3 is missing, install it free from https://www.python.org/downloads/macos/.
   You can also start manually from the folder with: python3 serve_studio.py

PHONE ON THE SAME WI-FI
Open Open on WiFi.command on your Mac. Open the phone address printed in Terminal.
Your Mac must stay awake and connected. Local HTTP phone microphone recording may be unavailable;
use the free computer voice, upload audio, or record on the Mac.
Projects are saved per browser/device; they do not sync automatically.

MAKE A VIDEO
1. Click Try the baby essentials example to load realistic photos, captions and prerecorded natural narration.
   Or write a title followed by one scene per line, then click Build scenes from my list.
   Example:
   My baby care video
   Mealtimes | A high chair, soft spoons, a bowl and washable bibs.
   Playtime | Sturdy books and large soft toys.
2. Select each scene. Upload your own JPG, PNG or WebP picture and edit its caption/narration.
3. Choose Free English computer voice and click Generate full voiceover.
   This voice is deliberately basic and synthetic. For a natural voice, use Record voice or Upload audio.
4. Preview the video and select Export video. Export prefers MP4 when your browser supports it;
   otherwise it uses WebM. Keep the tab visible during the real-time export.
5. The Mama's Love logo and mamaslove.ng are added to the frames when branding is enabled.
   Brand kit lets you change the website text.
6. Save project downloads a JSON backup. Audio and generated video clips are not included in that backup;
   use the browser's local project library or reattach narration after importing a backup.

PAID FEATURES
Optional cloud AI controls are collapsed and are not needed for this free workflow.
They remain optional, paid provider integrations. They do not become free by opening this editor.
The default workflow makes no backend API requests.

PRIVACY AND LICENSES
Imported images and narration stay in your current browser's project storage.
The example photography is AI-generated; its natural narration was generated offline.
The free computer voice uses meSpeak/eSpeak (GPL); its license, notices and complete original source package
are in vendor/mespeak/. The local fonts include their Open Font License files in fonts/.
Kokoro model attribution/license for the example narration is in media/Kokoro-LICENSE.txt.
