Yorùbá eulogy / praise names

Oríkì is more than a “cute meaning.”

Oríkì names are praise-names or cognomens. Some are tied to family, lineage, place, circumstance of birth or inherited praise traditions, so a short English gloss can never capture the whole cultural story.
